## Haseeb Javed

Haseeb Javed is a doctoral researcher in Computer Science and Engineering at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), South Korea, doing research in large language models for healthcare under the supervision of Prof. Farman Ali. His work focuses on privacy-preserving clinical consultation, parameter-efficient adaptation of language models, and robustness of medical machine-learning systems. He completed an M.S. in Electrical Engineering and a Master of Project Management at Muhammad Nawaz Sharif University of Engineering and Technology, Multan, and a B.S. in Electrical Engineering at the University of Engineering and Technology, Taxila. He has been supported by a Sungkyunkwan University full academic scholarship (2022–2026). His published research appears in IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics, Artificial Intelligence Review, Journal of Big Data, the AAAI Symposium Series, IEEE Access, Computing, Sensors, and Energies.
